# GAP CONTROLS integrated assurance program
Status: readiness program, not certified. Scope: entire application, supporting infrastructure, development factory, suppliers and operating organization. Existing test evidence is retained but does not confer conformity. Only independent certification bodies can issue certificates for applicable management systems. No certificate, mark, claim or paid engagement is authorized by this repository.

## Proposed benchmark set
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS) and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AIMS) are organizational certification targets. ISO/IEC 25010:2023 is the product quality model, not an automatic certificate. WCAG 2.2 AA is full-page and complete-process conformance. OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 Level 2 is the proposed technical verification baseline. NIST SSDF is a secure development framework. Saudi PDPL and implementing regulations are legal obligations subject to applicability analysis, not a certification. Core Web Vitals and cross-browser/localization are acceptance metrics. Use licensed normative ISO texts and the exact applicable legal requirements for a clause-level assessment; this program is a high-level mapping, not a substitute.

Sources: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001 ; https://www.iso.org/standard/42001 ; https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html ; https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ ; https://asvs.owasp.org/ ; https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final ; https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/ ; https://web.dev/articles/vitals .

## Execution
1. Establish approved legal entity, management scope, accountable sponsor, asset and supplier inventory, data map and risk criteria. Determine customer and regulatory requirements, including transfers for Saudi data. Assign named owners and independent auditors; role labels in the register are proposed, not appointments.
2. Build ISMS/AIMS documented policies, risk treatment, statement of applicability, records of processing, AI impact assessments, supplier due diligence, access controls, incident response and continuity plans. Validate them against licensed standards and local legal advice. Collect operational evidence, not merely templates.
3. For every page and API: test complete authorized journeys, accessibility, localization, data minimization, negative inputs, tenant boundaries, project permissions, recovery and audit. Include direct Supabase browser writes, storage, RLS, scheduled work and cloud infrastructure even though these are not page/API files. Real customer identity and dedicated full schema remain a prerequisite. No live customer records in current staging.
4. Fix high-risk issues, retest exact immutable commits, maintain test traceability, register risks and exceptions. Independent penetration test, accessibility assessment, restoration drill, red-team and review of all software dependencies and build provenance. Do not interpret zero audit findings as malware-free.
5. Management reviews and internal audits: verify corrective actions, management-system operation and records over a sufficient representative period agreed with auditor. Independent certification body with accreditation covering relevant standard/scope conducts Stage 1 and Stage 2 as applicable. Resolve findings and verify actual certificate scope and validity. Renew/surveillance according to certification agreement. No guaranteed outcome.
6. Keep production held until independently accepted. CI checks all app routes are registered, and refuses release for any unaccepted entry; this is a metadata completeness gate, not a signature or certificate validator. Branch protection and actual release enforcement must independently be configured and verified.

## Evidence dossier
For each requirement record source/version and clause, applicability rationale, owner, exact commit/configuration fingerprint, test procedure and result, artifact URL, risk, remediation, independent reviewer, date and expiry. Store sensitive audit materials privately, never on this public repository. Register contains only scope and status. Obtain certification quotes and approve cost separately.
